✦ Design Principles

  • Mobile-first. Every screen designed for iPhone SE first, then scaled up.
  • One tap to value. After AA consent, the dashboard must load in under 3 seconds.
  • No dead ends. Every error screen offers a clear next step.
  • No fake numbers. Empty states show real placeholders, not mock data.
  • Information, not advice. Every AI output ends with a one-line disclaimer.
  • Read-only framing. Reinforce that Finous cannot move money — the user stays in control.

FeatureWhy Not
Bill paymentsRequires additional licences (BBPS)
