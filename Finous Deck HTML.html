The Problem
Banks know the balance.
They don't know the customer.
01
Fragmented financial picture
A bank sees only what's in its own accounts. The customer's loans elsewhere, investments, credit card debt, real net worth — none of it visible. Cross‑sell becomes guesswork. Advice becomes generic.
02
Engagement is falling
Customers manage money across 6 to 8 apps. The bank is one of them, and often not the one they open first. Banks have no way to change that without understanding the full picture.
03
Building in‑house is not realistic
AA integration, financial dashboards, AI advisory — 12 to 18 months of engineering, ₹2 to 3 crore before anything works. Cooperative banks and mid‑size NBFCs do not have that runway. They need it ready.

The Moat
Building a defensible AI moat.

The Fine‑tuned Language Model (FLM) is our proprietary AI engine – built specifically for Indian personal finance and deployed in Year 2.

Why frontier LLMs fail
  • Cost explodes – at 5,000 MAU, API costs hit ₹7.5L/month – wiping out our entire Month 12 revenue.
  • Poor domain knowledge – they know 401(k)s and ISAs, not PPF, NPS, ELSS, or Section 80C.
  • Inconsistent Hindi – real Indian users code‑switch mid‑sentence; frontier models handle this poorly.
  • Privacy risk – every financial query travels to a third‑party server.
The Finous FLM solution
  • Fine‑tuned on 50,000+ proprietary examples – RBI/SEBI/IRDAI regulations + synthetic Q&A verified by finance professionals + anonymised real user interactions (with consent).
  • Reduces API costs by ~80% – from ₹7.5L/month to ₹20–40K/month at scale.
  • Native Hindi‑English handling – trained on real Indian code‑switched conversations.
  • Data stays on our infrastructure – DPDP Act 2023 compliant, no third‑party data leakage.
Data is the moat. Competitors can replicate AA integration – they cannot replicate our proprietary dataset of real Indian financial conversations.

Fine‑tune Llama/Mistral/Sarvam on 50K+ examples. One‑time cost: ₹2–5L.
