Data Usage and Sharing Policy

1. Purpose of This Policy

This Data Usage and Sharing Policy supplements our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. It provides detailed information about how Nidhiverse Private Limited ("Finous", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your data.

This policy is designed to comply with:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (DPDP Rules)
  • The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Account Aggregator framework
  • Other applicable Indian laws and regulations

2. Data We Collect and Why

The following table summarises the categories of data we collect and the purposes for which we collect it:

Data Category What We Collect Why We Collect It
Contact Data Name, email address, phone number Communication, account management, and customer support
Identity Data PAN, Aadhaar number, date of birth, address eKYC verification and regulatory compliance
Financial Data Bank balances, transactions, loans, investments, insurance Unified dashboard and AI insights (with consent via Account Aggregator)
Usage Data Device information, IP address, pages visited, time spent, click patterns Improving Services and personalising your experience
Communication Data Questions, feedback, support requests, Voice Agent conversations Responding to inquiries and improving AI models

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  1. Providing Services: To operate the Finous platform, unified dashboard, AI Advisor, Voice Agent, and other features
  2. Personalisation: To tailor insights, recommendations, and content to your financial situation and preferences
  3. Communication: To respond to your inquiries, send important updates, and provide customer support
  4. Improving Services: To enhance features, fix bugs, develop new functionality, and train AI models
  5. Regulatory Compliance: To meet legal and regulatory obligations under RBI guidelines, DPDP Act, and other applicable laws
  6. Security: To protect against fraud, unauthorised access, cyber threats, and ensure the integrity of our Services

4. How We Share Your Data

4.1 With Your Consent

We share your data with third parties only with your explicit, informed consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted service providers who help us operate the Services, including:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers: For hosting, storage, and computing resources
  • Account Aggregator entities: RBI-regulated entities that facilitate data access from Financial Information Providers (FIPs)
  • eKYC verification providers: For identity verification and KYC compliance
  • AI and machine learning providers: For processing data and generating insights
  • Analytics providers: For understanding usage patterns and improving Services

All service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the specified purposes.

4.3 For Legal and Regulatory Reasons

We may disclose data when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request. This includes compliance with RBI directives, court orders, and other legal obligations.

4.4 In Anonymised or Aggregated Form

We may share anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you for research, analytics, or improving our AI models. This data is stripped of personal identifiers and cannot be used to identify individuals.

6. Consent and Withdrawal

6.1 Obtaining Consent

We obtain your consent in a clear, specific, and informed manner:

  • Explicit: You must actively opt-in; we do not use pre-ticked boxes or implied consent
  • Informed: We clearly explain what data will be collected, why, and how it will be used
  • Specific: Consent is obtained for specific purposes, not blanket consent
  • Revocable: You can withdraw consent at any time

6.2 Withdrawing Consent

You have the right to withdraw consent at any time:

  • Via Account Aggregator app: Use the AA app to manage and revoke consent
  • Via email: Contact us at privacy@finous.site
  • Via the Services: Use the settings or account management features

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

6.3 Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to nominate: Nominate a person to exercise your rights on your behalf
  • Right to grievance redressal: Raise concerns or complaints about data processing

To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@finous.site.

7. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Access controls: Role-based access, multi-factor authentication, principle of least privilege
  • Regular audits: Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) by independent security firms
  • Employee training: Regular security awareness and data protection training
  • Incident response: Documented procedures for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and regulators in accordance with the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter
  • Financial data via AA: Retained only for the duration specified in the consent artefact
  • Communication data: Retained as needed for support and improvement purposes
  • Legal and regulatory data: Retained as required by applicable laws

Once data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

9. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request information about your personal data being processed
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to nominate: Nominate a person to exercise your rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity
  • Right to grievance redressal: Raise concerns or complaints about data processing

To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@finous.site. We will respond to your request within the timeframes specified by the DPDP Act.
