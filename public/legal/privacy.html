By using our Services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Identity information: PAN, Aadhaar number, date of birth, address (for eKYC verification)
  • Financial information: Any financial data you voluntarily provide
  • Communications: Questions, feedback, support requests, and Voice Agent conversations

2.2 Data Collected via Account Aggregator Framework

With your explicit consent, we access the following financial data through the RBI-regulated Account Aggregator network:

  • Bank account balances and transactions
  • Loan details and repayment history
  • Investment holdings (mutual funds, stocks, bonds)
  • Insurance policies
Important: All data access is consent-based and read-only. We cannot move, withdraw, or transact on your money. You can revoke consent at any time.

2.3 Data Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns
  • Cookies and similar technologies: For authentication, preferences, and analytics (see Section 8)

2.4 Data We Do Not Collect

  • Bank passwords, PINs, or private keys
  • Any data without your explicit consent
  • Data from children under 18 without parental consent

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Providing Services: To operate the Finous platform, unified dashboard, and AI Advisor
  • Personalisation: To tailor insights and recommendations to your financial situation
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries, send updates, and provide support
  • Improving Services: To enhance features, fix bugs, and develop new functionality
  • Regulatory compliance: To meet legal and regulatory obligations under RBI, DPDP Act, and other applicable laws
  • Security: To protect against fraud, unauthorised access, and cyber threats

We will not use your data for purposes other than those stated above without obtaining your explicit consent.

4. How We Share Your Data

4.1 With Your Consent

We share your data only with your explicit, informed consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted service providers who help us operate the Services, including:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers (hosting and storage)
  • Account Aggregator entities (RBI-regulated)
  • eKYC verification providers
  • AI and machine learning providers
  • Analytics providers

All service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the specified purposes.

4.3 For Legal and Regulatory Reasons

We may disclose data when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.

4.4 In Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you of any such change.

4.5 In Anonymised or Aggregated Form

We may share anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you for research, analytics, or improving our AI models.

5. How We Protect Your Data

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Access controls: Role-based access, multi-factor authentication, principle of least privilege
  • Regular audits: Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) by independent security firms
  • Employee training: Regular security awareness and data protection training
  • Incident response: Documented procedures for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and regulators in accordance with the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

Data collected via the Account Aggregator framework is retained only for the duration specified in the consent artefact. Once consent expires or is withdrawn, the data is deleted from our systems.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time via the Account Aggregator app or by contacting us
  • Right to nominate: Nominate a person to exercise your rights on your behalf
  • Right to grievance redressal: Raise concerns or complaints about data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@finous.site.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for authentication and security
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics cookies: Understand how you use our Services to improve them

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect Service functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored and processed in India. We do not transfer personal data outside India without appropriate safeguards and your consent, as required by the DPDP Act.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the Services. Your continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
