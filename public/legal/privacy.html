Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 4 November 2026 · Last Updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes what the Finous beta collects, why, who sees it, and what you can do about it. It covers the Finous app. We will update it before we add anything new that touches your data, such as connecting to your bank accounts.

1. Who We Are

Finous is run by Nidhiverse Private Limited, a company incorporated in India under the Companies Act, 2013, based in Pune, Maharashtra. For the personal data you give us, we are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

By creating an account and joining the beta, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Give Us

  • Your email address, to create your account and send you a sign-in code. If you add your name or city to your profile, we keep those too.
  • Financial details you choose to enter, such as accounts, balances, loans, goals and tax details, once those features are available to you.
  • Your questions to the in-app advisor, and any messages you send us.
  • Your consent records: when you accepted the beta terms and this policy, and which version.

2.2 Account Aggregator

Finous does not connect to the RBI Account Aggregator framework today, and we do not collect data from your banks. If that changes, we will ask for your separate, explicit consent first and update this policy before any data is collected.

Important: We never ask for your bank passwords, PINs or card numbers, and Finous cannot move, withdraw or transact on your money.

2.3 Data Collected Automatically

  • Technical data: your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider records in its server logs.
  • On your device: the app keeps your sign-in session, your language choice and, if you use it, the demo setting in your browser's local storage.

2.4 Data We Do Not Collect

  • Bank passwords, PINs or private keys
  • Your Aadhaar or PAN number
  • Data about children under 18

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service: to sign you in and show you what you have entered, with the calculations and recommendations the app provides
  • The advisor: to answer your questions using the figures you have entered
  • Communication: to send sign-in codes and important updates about your account
  • Security and legal duties: to protect against fraud and unauthorised access, and to meet our legal obligations

We will not use your data for any other purpose, and we will not use your conversations or details to train AI models, without separate, explicit consent from you.

4. How We Share Your Data

4.1 With Service Providers

We share data with providers that help us run Finous, only as far as their part of the work needs:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in. Your data is stored in its Mumbai, India region.
  • Vercel, which hosts the app.
  • Our email delivery provider, which sends sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Anthropic, whose AI model powers the advisor. When you ask the advisor a question, we send it the question and the figures it needs to answer.

Each provider is bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent terms to protect your data and use it only for the service it provides to us.

4.2 For Legal and Regulatory Reasons

We may share data when required by law, regulation or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Nidhiverse Private Limited, our users or others.

4.3 In Business Transfers

If Nidhiverse Private Limited is merged, acquired or sells its assets, your data may be transferred to the new owner. We will tell you before that happens.

4.4 Anonymised or Aggregated Form

We may share anonymised or aggregated data that cannot identify you.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit, and at rest by our database provider.
  • Separation: the database enforces rules that lock each person's data to their own account, so one user cannot read or change another's.
  • Limited access: only the people who need access to run the service can reach the systems that hold your data.

If there is a data breach that affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the DPDP Act and its rules require.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it together with everything linked to it, such as your accounts, goals, tax details and consent records. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time
  • Nominate: nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot
  • Grievance redressal: raise a concern about how we handle your data

To use any of these rights, email privacy@finous.site. Download and delete options inside the app are on their way.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Finous app keeps you signed in and remembers your language using your browser's local storage. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, which will sign you out.

9. Children's Privacy

Finous is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where Your Data Is Processed

Your account data is stored in India. The advisor is the exception: when you use it, your question and the figures it needs are processed by our AI provider outside India, under the agreement described above. If you do not want this, do not use the advisor.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we make a significant change, we will ask you to review and accept it again before you continue, and we will update the "Last Updated" date.
